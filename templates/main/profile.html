About Me

Dr. Danielle Svarovski is an experienced family psychologist with over 15 years of practice helping individuals, couples, and families navigate complex emotional and relational challenges. She began her career at the Vienna Family Counseling Center, where she worked for seven years, supporting families in conflict resolution, parenting guidance, and building healthy communication patterns. Later, she joined The European Institute for Mental Wellbeing, where she collaborated with multidisciplinary teams to develop therapeutic programs for families affected by trauma, loss, and major life transitions. Her expertise extends across diverse cultural and social backgrounds, having worked with clients in Austria, Germany, and Canada, both in private practice and community mental health organizations. Danielle is known for her empathetic, structured, and solution-focused approach, blending evidence-based methods such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), and Narrative Therapy.
